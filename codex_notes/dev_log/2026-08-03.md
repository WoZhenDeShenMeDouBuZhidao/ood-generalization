# FITCE Train-Standard-Deviation Scaling on the Top-5 Deficits

## Goal

Test whether the large balanced-metric FITCE deficits are caused by aligning
raw input gradients whose magnitudes depend on feature units. The experiment
changes only `importance_scale` from `none` to `train_std` on the five datasets
with the largest 2026-08-02 FITCE selection deficits.

## Settings

- Datasets: `acs/acspubliccoverage`, `acs/acsmobility`,
  `acs/acshealthinsurance`, `acs/acsincome`, and
  `tableshift/college_scorecard`.
- Both CE and FITCE use unified class reweighting `on`.
- `REPEAT=3`, seeds `9803`, `38224`, and `8113`; `PATIENCE=200`,
  `MAX_EPOCHS=5000`, `LR=1e-4`, train batch 256, and eval batch 2048.
- FITCE: GPT-5.4 semantic `rank/rank`, gradient term only,
  `REG_SCALE=0.1`, `tau=2`, no warmup, and `importance_scale=train_std`.
- Curves were generated for all three repeats. SHAP was not generated in this
  run, so its result JSONs have empty SHAP-distance fields.

All five candidate JSONs have `status=ok`, `data_cache_hit=true`, three
completed repeats, the expected seeds, and matching metadata.

## Results

`Base` is the preceding FITCE setting with `importance_scale=none`. ID is
validation Balanced Accuracy + Macro-F1. OOD Selection is BAcc OOD Mean +
BAcc OOD Worst + Macro-F1 OOD Mean + Macro-F1 OOD Worst.

| Dataset | ID TrainStd - Base | ID TrainStd - CE | OOD TrainStd - Base | OOD TrainStd - CE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `acs/acspubliccoverage` | +0.1880 | -0.0152 | +0.5250 | -0.0370 |
| `acs/acsmobility` | +0.2177 | +0.0012 | +0.2963 | +0.0586 |
| `acs/acshealthinsurance` | +0.0913 | -0.0101 | +0.0741 | +0.0046 |
| `acs/acsincome` | +0.0554 | -0.0087 | +0.0201 | -0.0202 |
| `tableshift/college_scorecard` | -0.0036 | -0.0011 | -0.0190 | -0.0474 |
| **Top-5 macro** | **+0.1098** | **-0.0068** | **+0.1793** | **-0.0083** |

Paired over the three seed-level top-5 macro averages, TrainStd versus CE is:

- ID delta: `-0.0068 +/- 0.0084`.
- OOD Selection delta: `-0.0083 +/- 0.0542`.

The top-5 aggregate is therefore statistically compatible with CE on both ID
and OOD after adding TrainStd scaling.

TrainStd removes the early FITCE checkpoints on the principal failures:

- all three `acspubliccoverage` repeats move from best epochs `61-162` to
  `448-879`, with consistent ID and OOD recovery;
- the `acsmobility` seed with best epoch 14 moves to epoch 175;
- the collapsed `acshealthinsurance` seed with best epoch 1 moves to epoch
  461 and recovers `+0.2769` ID and `+0.2289` OOD Selection.

## Interpretation

- Raw-gradient feature-scale mismatch is a major FITCE failure source. Scaling
  gradients by train standard deviation recovers most of the ID and OOD gap
  and removes the clearest early optimization collapses.
- `acspubliccoverage` is not fully solved: its residual deficits are `-0.0152`
  ID and `-0.0370` OOD Selection, but they are much smaller than the unscaled
  deficits of `-0.2032` and `-0.5620`.
- `college_scorecard` does not benefit from TrainStd. Its ID remains equivalent
  to CE, while its OOD result remains seed-sensitive and slightly worse in the
  mean.
- The next proposed screen is a smaller FITCE regularization scale while
  retaining TrainStd. That screen must include best-checkpoint SHAP distance
  and a CE-equivalent `REG_SCALE=0` attribution control; otherwise movement
  toward CE could be mistaken for useful FITCE regularization.
