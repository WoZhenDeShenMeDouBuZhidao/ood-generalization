# Synthetic Categorical-Integer FITCE Diagnosis

## Question

The categorical-only synthetic rerun showed that FITCE nearly matched the
remove-spurious optimum under one-hot encoding but remained unstable and below
the optimum under integer encoding. Rerun only `categorical_integer` CE and
FITCE with training curves, validation/test SHAP, and fixed-best-checkpoint
Grad-L2 diagnostics to determine whether the bad FITCE seed simply failed to
fit the oracle importance prior.

Before the rerun, clear the experiment `output` directory because its previous
formal results had already been recorded in the development logs. Preserve all
temporary random-ranking and analysis artifacts under `.codex_tmp`.

## Protocol

- Use the current categorical-causal schema, which removes original features
  `0` and `1`; the eight remaining model features contain only categorical
  causal signal, one continuous spurious feature, and noise.
- Use the same three model seeds `9803`, `38224`, and `8113`, full data,
  `reweighting=off`, learning rate `1e-4`, train/eval batches `256/2048`,
  patience `200`, and at most `5000` epochs.
- FITCE uses the exact oracle score prior, model-space all-feature alignment,
  gradient term only, `reg_scale=10`, no warmup, `tau=2`, and
  `importance_scale=train_std`.
- Record one training curve and six margin-SHAP plots per repeat: train/validation,
  ID test, and the four OOD environments. Also record raw margin Grad-L2 from
  each fixed best checkpoint.
- ID selection is ID balanced accuracy plus ID macro F1. OOD selection is the
  sum of OOD mean/worst balanced accuracy and macro F1.

The rerun reproduces the 8/26 CE and FITCE aggregate metrics to floating-point
summation tolerance (`max absolute difference <= 4.4e-16`).

## Evidence and Interpretation

- Seed `8113` again collapses. Its OOD selection is `1.1994` below seed `9803`,
  although it has a lower SHAP RankDist and nearly the same SHAP WeightDist.
  The failure is therefore not explained by simply failing to learn the
  oracle feature-importance ranking.
- At its best checkpoint, seed `8113` already assigns `99.39%` of raw Grad-L2
  to causal features. Suppressing spurious/noise gradients is not sufficient
  to recover the correct categorical decision function.
- The fixed-checkpoint total Grad-L2 is approximately `0.322` for seed `8113`,
  versus `0.967` and `1.305` for the two better seeds. Normalized-distribution
  alignment cannot detect this absolute-sensitivity difference.
- The seed-8113 validation metric peaks at epoch `44` and then drops sharply
  while the gradient-target loss continues toward approximately `1.08`; its
  performance does not recover. Better gradient-loss optimization is not
  monotonically related to better predictive performance in this case.
- Integer derivatives are particularly questionable for `causal_nominal`.
  Its label relation is non-monotonic across codes: categories `0/3` favor one
  class while `1/2` favor the other. Predictions at the valid category points
  do not determine the model derivative between those points.
- The existing one-hot result on the same semantic problem is stable and nearly
  optimal: FITCE OOD selection is `3.6246`, versus `3.6258` for remove
  spurious. This isolates integer categorical representation/alignment as the
  primary method concern rather than oracle-prior quality.

## Candidate Improvements

1. Replace local input derivatives for integer categorical features with a
   categorical-aware finite-difference or centered category-contrast signal;
   retain scaled margin Grad-L2 for continuous features.
2. Replace exact prior-distribution matching with a less invasive ranking or
   top-versus-tail margin objective that penalizes only ordering violations.
3. Test a regularization warmup as a controlled optimization-stability change.
4. If low-sensitivity failures remain after fixing the categorical signal, add
   a weak lower bound or anchor for total causal gradient/contrast magnitude.

The evidence prioritizes the categorical-aware signal. Warmup and magnitude
control address observed optimization symptoms but do not fix the semantic
invalidity of a local derivative through nominal integer codes.
