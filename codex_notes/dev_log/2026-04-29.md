## Hyperparameter Sweep

All rows below use structured GPT score weights from 2026-04-25:

```text
SCHL=9, WKHP=8, OCCP=8, AGEP=7, COW=6,
MAR=5, RELP=4, POBP=3, SEX=2, RAC1P=1
```

All rows use 3 seeds.

| config | changed setting | ID | OOD MEAN | OOD WORST |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `reg_0p25` | `REG_SCALE=0.25` | 0.8653 | 0.7582 | 0.7139 |
| `reg_0p75` | `REG_SCALE=0.75` | 0.8583 | 0.7599 | 0.7161 |
| `reg_1p0` | `REG_SCALE=1.0` | 0.8563 | 0.7618 | 0.7194 |
| `target_power_0p5` | `target_power=0.5` | 0.8695 | 0.7585 | 0.7103 |
| `target_power_1p5` | `target_power=1.5` | 0.8570 | 0.7601 | 0.7175 |
| `suppress_3` | `suppress_scale=3.0` | 0.8578 | 0.7598 | 0.7170 |
| `suppress_9` | `suppress_scale=9.0` | 0.8578 | 0.7598 | 0.7170 |
| `grad_1_weight_3` | `grad_scale=1.0`, `weight_scale=3.0` | 0.8690 | 0.7589 | 0.7126 |
| `grad_2_weight_1` | `grad_scale=2.0`, `weight_scale=1.0` | 0.8692 | 0.7568 | 0.7090 |

Interpretation:

- Increasing `REG_SCALE` from `0.5` to `1.0` was the best hyperparameter move:
  - OOD MEAN rose to `0.7618`.
  - OOD WORST rose to `0.7194`.
  - ID remained above CE reweighting.
- Lowering `REG_SCALE` to `0.25` made the model closer to CE and hurt OOD.
- Flattening target distribution with `target_power=0.5` raised ID but hurt OOD WORST badly.
- Sharpening with `target_power=1.5` was better than flattening but still below `REG_SCALE=1.0`.
- `suppress_scale` had no effect here because these structured score weights are all positive;
  no feature is in the suppressed mask.
- Reducing either gradient or weight target pressure increased ID but hurt OOD WORST, especially
  when `weight_scale` was reduced to `1.0`.

- The best feature-prior setting from this round is:
  - structured GPT score weights from 2026-04-25
  - `REG_SCALE=1.0`
  - `reweighting=True`
  - `importance_scale="train_std"`
  - `grad_scale=2.0`

  - `target_power=1.0`
- It improves over the 2026-04-25 feature-prior setting:
  - ID is lower by `0.0038`
  - OOD MEAN is higher by `0.0022`
  - OOD WORST is higher by `0.0042`
- It beats CE reweighting on:
  - ID: `0.8553 > 0.8490`
  - OOD MEAN: `0.7624 > 0.7580`
- It still does not beat CE reweighting on OOD WORST:
  - `0.7212 < 0.7232`
- Reducing `OCCP` to `6` helps OOD WORST compared with the 2026-04-25 feature-prior base,
  but does not beat the `REG_SCALE=1.0` confirm.
- Current bottleneck is therefore not merely `OCCP` mass or synthetic hyperparameter transfer.
  The limiting issue is worst-state robustness under the GPT prior. The feature-prior loss can
  now improve ID and OOD MEAN over CE reweighting, but CE reweighting remains stronger on the
  single worst state.
