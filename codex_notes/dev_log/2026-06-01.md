### LLM Attribution Regularizer Optimization

Goal: optimize the LAAT-style MLP baseline implemented by `run_acs_laat.py`.

Optimization order:

1. Fix the ranking method first at `REG_SCALE=10`.
2. Compare `RANKING_METHOD=score_all` vs `RANKING_METHOD=score`, both with
   `FEATURE_WEIGHT_MODE=score`.
3. After selecting the better ranking method, sweep `REG_SCALE` for that
   selected method.

Selection score is:

`Acc OOD MEAN + Acc OOD WORST + F1 OOD MEAN + F1 OOD WORST`.

#### Out-of-Order Score-All REG_SCALE Sweep

These runs were started before the ranking-method gate above. They are retained
as exploratory results, but should not drive the final LAAT optimization path
because `score_all + REG_SCALE=10` did not win the ranking-method comparison.

Exploratory sweep settings:

- `RANKING_METHOD=score_all`
- `FEATURE_WEIGHT_MODE=score`
- `REG_SCALE in {10, 100, 1000, 10000}`
- `REPEAT=5`

Rationale: LAAT uses MSE between normalized attribution probabilities and LLM
feature-score probabilities. The raw MSE magnitude is much smaller than CE, so a
log-scale search is more appropriate than a small linear grid. `REG_SCALE=0` is
covered by the existing MLP CrossEntropyLoss benchmark and is used as a reference
rather than rerun here.
