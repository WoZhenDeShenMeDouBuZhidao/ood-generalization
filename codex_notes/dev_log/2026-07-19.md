## Synthetic OOD FITCE First-Layer-Weight-Only Sweep

Goal: test whether FITCE's first-layer-weight term alone can suppress
`spurious_continuous_2p25` and approach the direct remove-spurious oracle on
`synthetic_ood`.

Settings:

- Dataset: `synthetic_ood/simple`.
- Prior: synthetic oracle `score_all` / `score`.
- Method: `fitce`.
- Weight-only loss: `loss_lambda=1`, `loss_alpha=0`, so
  `grad_scale=0`, `weight_scale=1`.
- `importance_scale=none`, `suppress_bound=0`,
  `grad_prob_temperature=1`.
- `REG_SCALE`: `0.1`, `1`, `10`, then extended to `20`, `50`, `100`,
  `200` because the best result was initially at the sweep boundary.
- `REPEAT=3`, `PATIENCE=100`, `LR=1e-4`.
- SHAP sample size: `500`.
- Plots: curve and SHAP enabled for every run.
- CPU thread limit per process.

- Total generated plots: 42
  (`7` reg scales x `3` repeats x curve/SHAP).

Reference rows from `2026-07-09`:

- MLP CE: selection `2.8153`, OOD Acc `0.7073`, OOD F1 `0.7003`.
- Nonnegative LAAT best: `REG_SCALE=0.1`, selection `2.8551`,
  OOD Acc `0.7157`, OOD F1 `0.7118`.
- FITCE grad-only best: `REG_SCALE=10`, `grad_prob_temperature=2`,
  selection `3.7270`, OOD Acc `0.9319`, OOD F1 `0.9316`.
- Direct remove-spurious oracle: selection `3.7279`, OOD Acc `0.9322`,
  OOD F1 `0.9317`.

Conclusion:

- The first-layer-weight term alone is useful: high regularization raises OOD
  Acc/F1 from the MLP CE baseline around `0.707/0.700` to about
  `0.887/0.886`.
- It does not approach the direct remove-spurious oracle as closely as the
  optimized grad-only term. Best weight-only selection is `3.5455`, still about
  `0.1824` below remove-spurious and `0.1815` below grad-only `tau=2`.
- Performance saturates from `REG_SCALE=100` to `200`
  (`3.5441` -> `3.5455`), so there is no strong reason to keep expanding
  weight-only regularization.
- The first-layer weight distribution distance is not monotonic with
  performance: low `REG_SCALE=1` has the lowest First WeightDist (`0.1049`) but
  poor OOD performance. The useful signal seems to come from stronger weight
  pressure changing the learned solution, not simply minimizing first-layer
  distance in isolation.
