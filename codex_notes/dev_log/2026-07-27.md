# FITCE Real-World REG_SCALE, Temperature, and Warmup Screening

## Goal

Test whether the real-world FITCE performance gap can be reduced by:

1. lowering `REG_SCALE`;
2. lowering the gradient-probability temperature;
3. linearly warming `REG_SCALE` from zero.

Hyperparameters are selected only on the 18 real-world datasets. Synthetic OOD
is excluded from this optimization.

## Shared Settings

- Datasets: 8 ACS, 2 WHYSHIFT, and 8 TableShift datasets.
- `REPEAT=3`; model seeds: `9803`, `38224`, and `8113`.
- `PATIENCE=200`, `MAX_EPOCHS=5000`, `LR=1e-4`.
- `TRAIN_BATCH=256`, `EVAL_BATCH=2048`.
- Dataset-specific `reweighting=auto`.
- FITCE uses the gradient term only: `loss_lambda=1`, `loss_alpha=1`.
- `importance_scale=train_std`, `suppress_bound=0`.
- GPT prior: `gpt-5.4`, semantic feature space, `ranking_method=rank`,
  `feature_weight_mode=rank`.
- No curve or SHAP plots.
- Selection score:
  `Acc OOD Mean + Acc OOD Worst + F1 OOD Mean + F1 OOD Worst`.

Metric cells are macro means across 18 datasets. The displayed confidence
range is the mean dataset-level 95% Student-t confidence interval range over
the three seeds.

- The interrupted `REG_SCALE=0.01/0.03` extension was stopped, deleted, and is
  intentionally excluded.

### Finding

Lowering tau does not improve real-world performance. Selection falls by
`0.0287` at `tau=1` and by `0.0477` at `tau=0.5`, relative to `tau=2`.
Even `tau=2` remains `0.0192` below MLP CE. The warmup experiment therefore
keeps `tau=2`.

## REG_SCALE Warmup

### Implementation

- Added `--reg-warmup-epochs`.
- A positive value linearly increases the criterion's effective REG_SCALE:

  `effective_reg_scale(epoch) = REG_SCALE * min(epoch / warmup_epochs, 1)`.

- The first epoch uses zero regularization.
- The configured full scale is reached after the requested warmup duration.
- Each repeat records the effective REG_SCALE at its selected checkpoint.
- Default `0` preserves constant regularization.
- Compile, CLI, and small synthetic smoke checks passed.

The formal sweep fixes `REG_SCALE=0.1` and `tau=2`. The `warmup=0` row reuses
the constant-regularization result, and MLP CE reuses the matching
`patience_screen_p200_mlp_ce_r3` result from `2026-07-20.md`.

## Conclusions

- Lowering REG_SCALE is the main improvement. Moving from `10` to `0.1`
  raises macro selection from `2.3286` to `2.5431`.
- Lowering tau is harmful. `tau=2` remains the best tested value.
- Warmup gives a small, consistent improvement over constant
  `REG_SCALE=0.1`; `warmup=200` is best at `2.5505`.
- Warmup does not beat MLP CE, which remains at `2.5623`.
- The `warmup=200` gain should not be interpreted as successful training under
  full `REG_SCALE=0.1`: 29/54 checkpoints are selected before warmup completes,
  and the mean selected effective scale is only `0.0689`.
- This indicates that much of the warmup gain comes from weakening the
  regularizer at the selected checkpoint. It narrows, but does not resolve, the
  real-world FITCE gap.
- No warmup beyond 200 is run because the warmup already matches the configured
  patience and most selected checkpoints would spend still more of training
  below the intended regularization strength.
