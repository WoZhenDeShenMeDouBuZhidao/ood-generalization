# GPT and CE Prior Quality Against Random Rankings

## Protocol

- All methods are evaluated on the same 18 real-world datasets. FITCE uses
  auto categorical encoding, semantic expansion `split`, model-space gradient
  alignment, `importance_scale=train_std`, `reg_scale=0.1`, `tau=2`, class
  reweighting with scope `all`, the gradient term only, and three repeats.
- Current CE is the reproducible first three matched seeds from the five-repeat
  CE Grad-L2 run. The deleted historical 7/30 CE artifact is not used here.
- Random is the distribution of 301 random-ranking FITCE results per dataset.
  PR is the empirical percentile in that dataset's distribution. The summary
  reports Mean Dataset PR, the direct mean of the 18 PR values below.
- CE normalized mean normalizes each repeat's best-checkpoint raw Grad-L2 to
  sum to one, then averages across five repeats, or ten for the three datasets
  with lower stability. Stability rho is shown once in the ID table and also
  applies to the corresponding OOD row.
- CE mean rank converts each repeat's Grad-L2 vector to unique descending
  ordinal ranks, averages ranks across the same repeats, and uses the resulting
  unique ordering as a `rank/rank` FITCE prior.
- Per-dataset cells use `selection / PR`.
