- LAAT gives better downstream performance under this fair alignment sweep.

### Conclusion

The experiment separates alignment behavior from performance:

- If the claim is that the loss enforces the gradient prior, FITCE is much
  stronger than LAAT at the global-best-reg settings. FITCE wins Grad
  RankDist on 18 / 18 datasets and Grad WeightDist on 18 / 18 datasets.
- SHAP remains split when RankDist and WeightDist are kept separate. FITCE wins
  SHAP RankDist on 13 / 18 datasets, while LAAT wins SHAP WeightDist on
  11 / 18 datasets.
- If the objective is downstream OOD performance, LAAT is still better in this
  sweep: LAAT wins the global-best-reg selection score on 12 / 18 datasets.

For writing, the cleanest statement is: FITCE is better at controlling the
training-time gradient attribution target, but that does not automatically
translate into better SHAP distribution matching or OOD performance under this
fair LAAT comparison.
