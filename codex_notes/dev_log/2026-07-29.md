# CE and FITCE Reweighting Screening

## Goal

Re-evaluate class reweighting separately for MLP CE and FITCE. The previous
dataset defaults were selected using CE accuracy only, so they do not
necessarily maximize the current OOD selection objective or transfer to FITCE.

## Settings

- Datasets: 18 real-world datasets (8 ACS, 2 WHYSHIFT, 8 TableShift).
- Reweighting: explicit `off` versus `on`; `auto` is not used.
- `REPEAT=3`; model seeds: `9803`, `38224`, and `8113`.
- `PATIENCE=200`, `MAX_EPOCHS=5000`, `LR=1e-4`.
- `TRAIN_BATCH=256`, `EVAL_BATCH=2048`.
- No curve or SHAP plots.
- Selection score:
  `Acc OOD Mean + Acc OOD Worst + F1 OOD Mean + F1 OOD Worst`.
- FITCE: `gpt-5.4`, semantic feature space, `rank/rank`,
  `loss_lambda=1`, `loss_alpha=1` (gradient term only), `REG_SCALE=0.1`,
  `tau=2`, `warmup=0`, `importance_scale=none`, and `suppress_bound=0`.

All 72 result files have `status=ok`, three completed repeats, and matching
configuration metadata.

Metric cells below are macro means across 18 datasets. Confidence ranges are
the mean dataset-level 95% Student-t confidence interval ranges over the three
seeds.

## Frozen CE-Calibrated Policy

For all methods that support class reweighting, use one shared dataset-level
setting calibrated once with MLP CE:

`delta = CE selection(reweighting=on) - CE selection(reweighting=off)`

- Set reweighting to `off` when `delta < -0.02`.
- Otherwise set reweighting to `on`.
- Freeze this choice before comparing additional baselines; FITCE and later
  methods do not receive method-specific reweighting optimization.

The `0.02` practical-equivalence margin gives the same map for any threshold
from `0.01` through `0.04`. The largest retained negative CE delta is `-0.0050`
(`acspubliccoverage`), while the smallest disabled delta is `-0.0433`
(`college_scorecard`).

Final dataset settings:

- `off`: `acshealthinsurance`, `acsmobility`, `college_scorecard`, `anes`,
  `brfss_blood_pressure`.
- `on`: the other 13 real-world datasets.
- These values were synchronized to the real-world `DatasetSpec.reweighting`
  auto defaults in `src/benchmark_config.py` on 2026-07-30.

When reporting this policy, calibration should use a development/validation
selection score rather than an untouched final test set. The choices recorded
here are now frozen and must not be revised using later baseline outcomes.

- The five worst FITCE datasets have median minority share `36.84%` and median

- Three of the five worst datasets are only mildly imbalanced:

- Imbalance can still interact with optimization, as shown by
  `acshealthinsurance`, but it cannot explain the cross-dataset ranking or the

### Diagnostic Priorities

1. `acspubliccoverage`: primary general FITCE failure. All six ID/OOD
   accuracy/F1 deltas are negative, the selection deficit is
   `-0.6937 +/- 0.1735`, and both methods use reweighting.
2. `acshealthinsurance`: primary reweighting-interaction and optimization
   failure. The deficit is `-0.3037 +/- 0.0599`, ID F1 falls by `0.2071`, and

3. `acstraveltime`: secondary F1-focused failure. OOD worst accuracy improves
   by `0.0344`, but ID/OOD F1 declines; the overall deficit is smaller and its
   interval crosses zero.
4. `acsincome`: secondary moderate, broad degradation under the common `on`
   setting. It is preferable to `brfss_blood_pressure` as a diagnostic target
   because the latter has a much wider paired interval.

## Conclusions

- Forced reweighting improves macro selection by `+0.0408` for CE and
  `+0.1048` for FITCE, mainly through F1 gains; mean accuracy is slightly
  lower.
- A global on/off choice is inferior to method-specific, dataset-specific
  selection. The best observed mean selection is `2.5721` for CE and `2.5255`
  for FITCE.
- Reweighting is method-dependent: CE and FITCE disagree on 6/18 datasets.
  A single `DatasetSpec.reweighting` value cannot represent both methods'
  observed optima.
- The frozen CE-calibrated shared policy deliberately prioritizes comparison
  fairness over each method's observed optimum. It gives selection `2.5716`
  for CE and `2.5172` for FITCE.
- FITCE remains below CE even after selecting each method's preferred
  reweighting (`2.5255` versus `2.5721`).
- Because most paired intervals cross zero at `REPEAT=3`, the mean choices are
  suitable as screening decisions, but small differences should not be
  presented as statistically conclusive.
