# Balanced-Metric FITCE Minus CE by Dataset

## Settings

- Results are from the 2026-07-30 real-world balanced-metric reproduction on
  the remote machine.
- Datasets: all 18 real-world datasets; synthetic datasets are excluded.
- Methods: MLP CE and FITCE, both with unified class reweighting `on`.
- `REPEAT=3`, seeds `9803`, `38224`, and `8113`; `PATIENCE=200`,
  `MAX_EPOCHS=5000`, `LR=1e-4`, train batch 256, and eval batch 2048.
- FITCE: GPT-5.4 semantic `rank/rank`, gradient term only,
  `REG_SCALE=0.1`, `tau=2`, no importance scaling, and no warmup.

## FITCE Minus CE by Dataset

`BAcc OOD Mean + BAcc OOD Worst + Macro-F1 OOD Mean + Macro-F1 OOD Worst`.

FITCE selection gain. Negative delta cells with a drop greater than `0.05`

CE Selection, FITCE Selection, and Selection Delta cells are all bolded.

## Summary

- FITCE has lower mean selection than CE on 11 of 18 datasets and higher
  selection on 7 of 18. The macro selection delta is `-0.0510`.
- `acspubliccoverage` remains the dominant FITCE failure: selection decreases
  by `0.5620`, and all six ID/OOD metric deltas drop by more than `0.05`.
- `acsmobility` has the second-largest mean deficit (`-0.2377`); five of its
  six ID/OOD metric deltas drop by more than `0.05`.
- Imbalance does not order the deficits. The most imbalanced dataset,
  `nhanes_lead` (`38.37:1`), is nearly neutral, while the largest failure,
  `acspubliccoverage`, is only mildly imbalanced (`1.71:1`).
