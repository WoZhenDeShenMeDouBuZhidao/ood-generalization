## MLP CE Reweighting Policy: WHYSHIFT and TableShift

Goal: decide whether future baselines that support class/sample reweighting
should enable it for WHYSHIFT and TableShift datasets.

Settings:

- Runner: `run_mlp_ce.py`
- Compared `--reweighting off` vs `--reweighting on`
- Benchmarks: `whyshift`, `tableshift`
- Common neural settings:
  - `REPEAT=5`
  - `PATIENCE=500`
  - `MAX_EPOCHS=5000`
  - `LR=1e-4`
  - `TRAIN_BATCH=256`
  - `EVAL_BATCH=2048`
  - `MODEL_SEEDS=[9803, 38224, 8113, 4854, 98825]`
- Dataset default caps from `src/benchmark_config.py`:
  - WHYSHIFT: `MAX_TRAIN_VAL_SIZE=8000`, `MAX_PER_TEST_SIZE=8000`
  - TableShift: `MAX_TRAIN_VAL_SIZE=10000`, `MAX_PER_TEST_SIZE=0`
- Policy selection score:
  `Acc OOD MEAN + Acc OOD WORST + F1 OOD MEAN + F1 OOD WORST`

## FITCE and LAAT Expanded-Suite Re-Optimization

Goal: re-optimize `FeatureImportanceTargetCELoss` and the LAAT-style
attribution regularizer after expanding from ACS-only to ACS + WHYSHIFT +
TableShift + synthetic_ood.

Optimization selection score:

`Acc OOD MEAN + Acc OOD WORST + F1 OOD MEAN + F1 OOD WORST`.

Selection excludes `synthetic_ood`; it is only run after the real-world
benchmarks select a best setting. This avoids choosing hyperparameters that
mainly exploit the synthetic environment.

Shared protocol:

- Model: PyTorch MLP
- `RANKING_MODEL=gpt-5.4`
- `REPEAT=5`
- `PATIENCE=500`
- `MAX_EPOCHS=5000`
- `LR=1e-4`
- `TRAIN_BATCH=256`
- `EVAL_BATCH=2048`
- Model seeds: `[9803, 38224, 8113, 4854, 98825]`
- Dataset caps and reweighting policy come from `src/benchmark_config.py`.
- Real-world optimization datasets: 8 ACS + 2 WHYSHIFT + 8 TableShift.
- Boundary rule: if a sweep's best setting is on a planned boundary, expand the
  sweep outward before treating it as final.
